## English Summary

We study normalized solutions to the Born--Infeld problem   \begin{equation*}   -{\rm div}\left(\frac{\nabla u}{\sqrt{1-|\nabla u|^2}}\right)+μu=f(u)\ \hbox{ in }\mathbb{R}^N,\qquad \int_{\mathbb{R}^N}|u|^2\,dx=2m^2,   \end{equation*}   $N\ge3$, where $m&gt;0$ is prescribed and $μ&gt;0$ is an unknown Lagrange multiplier. The nonlinearity $f$ is assumed to be superlinear at infinity, with no upper restriction on its growth, and to behave like $|s|^{p-2}s$ near zero, with $2&lt;p&lt;2^*$. Following a Lagrangian approach, we relate the action ground state level $a(μ)$ of the prescribed frequency problem to the masses of its ground states by means of one-sided derivative formulas, which lead to the existence of normalized solutions. A distinctive feature of the Born--Infeld operator is that the gradient constraint makes the energy bounded from below on every mass sphere, even in the $L^2$-supercritical case. For $2+4/N\le p&lt;2^*$ we obtain a threshold $c_*&gt;0$ such that normalized action ground states exist for $m^2&gt;c_*$, and none exist for $m^2&lt;c_*$. In the $L^2$-subcritical case they exist for every $m^2&gt;0$. We prove that every action ground state has constant sign and is radial up to a translation.
