## English Summary

For the Kostlan-Shub-Smale field $X$ of degree $k\ge3$ on the sphere $\mathbb{S}^{d-1}$, we prove the two-sided bound $(1-\varepsilon(u))\,δ_{*}(u)\le\mathbb{P}\{\sup X&gt;u\}\leδ_{*}(u)$ at every finite $(k,d)$, where $δ_{*}(u)$ is the expected number of critical points above $u$, halved when $k$ is even, and $\varepsilon(u)$ is explicit for $ρu\ge\min\{d-1,\frac32\sqrt{2(d-1)}\}$. The gap is therefore at most of order $e^{-(1+ρ^{2})u^{2}/2}$ with $ρ^{2}=k/(2(k-1))$. We also give $δ_{*}$ itself in closed form, as a finite sum of Hermite polynomials, together with a two-term envelope that is asymptotically sharp and a single-monomial relaxation of the form $Cu^{d-2}e^{-u^{2}/2}$. As an application we bracket the annealed complexity of the spherical $k$-spin Hamiltonian at every finite $(k,d)$: the upper bound holds at every energy, a lower bound of the same form at every energy above $9/4$ times the spectral edge $\sqrt{2(d-1)}/ρ$, above twice the edge when $d\ge10$, and is consistent with the Auffinger-Ben Arous-Černý complexity function as $d\to\infty$.
