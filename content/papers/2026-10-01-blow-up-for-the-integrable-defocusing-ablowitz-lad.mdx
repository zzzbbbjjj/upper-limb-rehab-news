## 中文摘要

这项工作为无限和有限晶格上的完全可积散焦 Ablowitz-Ladik 方程建立了有限时间放大。我们首先在均匀有界序列的空间 $\ell^\infty$ 中建立任意初始数据的局部适定性，没有衰减假设并且在一般边界条件下。然后，我们证明单位圆充当晶格动力学的分界线：只要解存在，单位圆内部、之上或外部具有初始模量的位点就将其模量保持在各自的区域内。因此，$\ell^\infty$ 单位球闭包中的初始数据生成全局实时解。另一方面，单位圆分离线开启了有限时间爆炸的可能性，这仅限于初始模量位于单位圆之外的位置。我们通过构造一类初始数据来建立这种场景，其相应的解决方案是奇异的，并且明确给出了精确的爆炸时间...

## English Summary

This work establishes finite-time blow-up for the completely integrable defocusing Ablowitz-Ladik equation on both infinite and finite lattices. We first establish local well-posedness for arbitrary initial data in the space $\ell^\infty$ of uniformly bounded sequences, with no decay assumptions and under general boundary conditions. We then show that the unit circle acts as a separatrix for the lattice dynamics: sites with an initial modulus inside, on, or outside the unit circle maintain their modulus within their respective region for as long as the solution exists. As a consequence, initial data in the closure of the $\ell^\infty$ unit ball generate global-in-time solutions. On the other hand, the unit circle separatrix opens up the possibility for finite-time blow-up, which is restricted to sites with initial modulus outside the unit circle. We establish this scenario by constructing a class of initial data whose corresponding solutions are singular, with explicitly given exact blow-up times. We also construct solutions emanating from initial data with an arbitrarily large single-site excitation outside the unit circle that exist globally in time, a behavior driven by the convergence of the corresponding modulus to the unit circle equilibrium. In the presence of such solutions, along with other important classes of analytical, global-in-time solutions that remain outside the closed $\ell^\infty$ unit ball for all times, we examine the fundamental question of the dominant dynamics for general initial data outside the closed unit ball. By utilizing the constructed class of singular solutions as subsolutions for the general problem, we prove that the dynamics for the completely integrable defocusing Ablowitz-Ladik lattice is dominated by finite-time blow-up. We conclude with numerical studies of illustrative examples of general initial data
