## 中文摘要

傅里叶准晶体是非周期结构，其衍射光谱不像普通准晶体那样由一组密集的布拉格峰组成，而是由分散在离散的非周期组中的孤立的布拉格峰组成。除了少数选定的波矢之外，这种稀疏的倒易空间结构应该使波传输基本上不受干扰。我们使用二维傅立叶准晶体散射的表面水波来测试这一预测。随着入射波矢的增加，全场测量揭示了三种不同的输运方式：透明度、选择性散射和强散射。通过从测量的波场重建结构因子，我们直接将这些状态与底层的倒易空间结构联系起来。我们的结果将傅里叶准晶体确立为一个物理平台，在该平台中，可以通过倒易空间中衍射峰的组织来控制波传输。

## English Summary

Fourier quasicrystals are aperiodic structures whose diffraction spectrum consists not of a dense set of Bragg peaks, as in ordinary quasicrystals, but of isolated ones scattered across a discrete, nonperiodic set. This sparse reciprocal-space structure should leave wave transport largely undisturbed except at a few selected wavevectors. We put this prediction to the test using surface water waves scattering off a two-dimensional Fourier quasicrystal. Full-field measurements reveal three distinct transport regimes as the incident wavevector increases: transparency, selective scattering, and strong scattering. By reconstructing the structure factor from the measured wavefields, we directly relate these regimes to the underlying reciprocal-space structure. Our results establish Fourier quasicrystals as a physical platform in which wave transport can be controlled through the organization of diffraction peaks in reciprocal space.
