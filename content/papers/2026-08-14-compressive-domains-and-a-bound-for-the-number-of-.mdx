## 中文摘要

我们引入了“压缩域”的概念，用于有理函数在完全非平凡值代数封闭非阿基米德域上的贝尔科维奇射影线上的作用。我们证明这样的域总是包含一个经典的不动点，并且我们利用这一事实为有理函数的固定轨迹的连通分量的数量给出一个尖锐的上限。我们给出了多项式函数的第二个证明，该证明使用了先前未发表的 Rivera-Letelier 质量公式。最后，我们给出了压缩域内关键权重的显式公式，作为经典不动点和边界点数量的函数。

## English Summary

We introduce the notion of a "compressive domain" for the action of a rational function on the Berkovich projective line over a complete nontrivially-valued algebraically closed nonarchimedean field. We prove that such a domain always contains a classical fixed point, and we leverage this fact to give a sharp upper bound for the number of connected components of the fixed locus of a rational function. We give a second proof for polynomial functions that uses a previously unpublished mass formula of Rivera-Letelier. Finally, we give an explicit formula for the crucial weight inside a compressive domain as a function of the number of classical fixed points and boundary points.
