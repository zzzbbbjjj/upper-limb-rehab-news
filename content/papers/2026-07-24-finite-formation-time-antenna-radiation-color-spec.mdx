## 中文摘要

我们计算了彩色单线态夸克反夸克对的介质内软胶子发射对平方矩阵元素的直接贡献。为此，我们结合了天线有限形成时间内发生的介质相互作用，从而解释了相对于真空基线的很大程度上被忽视的修改源。结果，出现了与天线的观众（即非发射）腿的重要相关性，大大增加了结果的复杂性，即使在前导 $N_c$ 阶也是如此。观察到的修改需要显着偏离瞬时天线形成的极限，其中计算有效地简化为众所周知的 BDMPS-Z 频谱。

## English Summary

We compute the direct contribution to the squared matrix element for the in-medium soft gluon emission off a color-singlet quark-antiquark pair. To do this, we incorporate medium interactions that take place during the finite formation time of the antenna, thereby accounting for a largely neglected source of modifications with respect to the vacuum baseline. As a consequence, non-trivial correlations with the spectator (i.e.\ non-emitting) leg of the antenna emerge, greatly increasing the complexity of the result even at leading-$N_c$ order. The observed modifications entail a significant departure from the limit of instantaneous antenna formation, where the calculation effectively reduces to the well-known BDMPS-Z spectrum.
