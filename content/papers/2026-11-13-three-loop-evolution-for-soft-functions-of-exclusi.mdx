## English Summary

We bootstrap the complete three-loop Lange--Neubert kernel for the leading-twist $B$-meson light-cone distribution amplitude in heavy quark effective theory by taking advantage of the conformal symmetry technique. In particular, we provide for the first time an all-order Wilson-line argument for the determination of the constant term in the LN kernel in terms of the light-quark, heavy-quark, and threshold soft anomalous dimensions. Remarkably, this innovative construction requires no additional three-loop diagrammatic calculations. Taking into account further the three-loop QCD evolutions for the hard and hard-collinear coefficient functions, the newly derived three-loop Lange--Neubert equation enables us to manifestly demonstrate the renormalization-group invariance of the soft-collinear factorization formula for the radiative leptonic $B_{u}^- \to γ\ell^{-} \bar ν_{\ell}$ decay at leading power, validating our bootstrapped three-loop kernel and more importantly, our full bootstrap approach.
