## 中文摘要

众所周知，聚合物玻璃的物理老化会增加其屈服应力并影响其失效行为。研究表明，与没有应力的时效（静态时效）相比，时效期间施加中等水平的应力会导致更高的屈服应力。这被解释为压力会加速身体衰老，并被描述为过度衰老。在这项研究中，我们在应力下老化 PMMA 玻璃，并使用探针重定向技术直接测量老化过程中和老化后的分段动力学。我们观察到，在应力下老化的样品尽管具有较高的屈服应力，但在应力释放后比静态老化的样品具有更快的分段动力学。对于这里探讨的条件范围，这与过度老化的解释相矛盾。我们的结果表明屈服应力不是结构松弛时间的简单函数，基于这种理解的理论模型需要修改。

## English Summary

It is well known that physical aging of polymer glasses increases their yield stress and affects their failure behavior. Studies indicate that application of moderate levels of stress during aging results in higher yield stress compared to aging in the absence of stress (quiescent aging). This has been interpreted to indicate that stress accelerates physical aging, and has been described as overaging. In this study, we age PMMA glasses under stress, and carry out direct measurement of segmental dynamics during and after aging by using a probe reorientation technique. We observe that samples aged under stress, despite having higher yield stress, have faster segmental dynamics after stress release than quiescently aged samples. This contradicts the overaging interpretation, for the range of conditions explored here. Our results indicate that yield stress is not a simple function of structural relaxation time and theoretical models based on this understanding need to be revised.
