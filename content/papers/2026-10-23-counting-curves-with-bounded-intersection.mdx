## 中文摘要

本文对双曲曲面上的曲线和多重曲线进行了计数，提供了显式上限，该上限以多项式方式依赖于长度，并以指数方式依赖于自交上限。

## English Summary

This paper counts curves, and multicurves, on hyperbolic surfaces, providing explicit upper bounds that depend polynomially on length and exponentially in terms of an upper bound on self-intersection.
