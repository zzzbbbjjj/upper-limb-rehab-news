## 中文摘要

某些表面上确定的理论，特别是波姆力学和动力学理论，需要“随机”初始条件才能实现其目标。我分析了最近的建议，即这个想法应该使用算法随机性来实现，被视为最初用于理解这些理论的典型性论证的数学强化。一方面，我的分析包括对这些论点的批评，因此也对这种尖锐化进行了辩护。另一方面，我认为柴廷随机序列的不完备性定理使得动力学理论不可能处理超出某些有限（但不是任意）数字的给定随机初始条件，或者以任意精度陈述这些条件而不损害其随机性（这是同一枚硬币的两面）。这挑战了这些理论做出除通常意义上的预测（如宏观陈述）之外的任何其他预测的能力，并将其所谓的决定论推上了形而上学的阶梯。

## English Summary

Certain prima facie deterministic theories, notably Bohmian mechanics and kinetic theory, require ``random'' initial conditions in order to achieve their goals. I analyze recent proposals that this idea should be implemented using algorithmic randomness, seen as a mathematical sharpening of the typicality arguments originally used to understand these theories. On the one hand my analysis includes a critique of these arguments and hence a defense of this sharpening. On the other hand, I argue that Chaitin's incompleteness theorems for random sequences make it impossible for a dynamical theory to either process given random initial conditions beyond some finite (but not arbitrary) number of their digits, or else to state such conditions with arbitrary precision without compromising their randomness (these are two sides of the same coin). This challenges the ability of such theories to make any other predictions than those that hold typically in the usual sense of the word (like macroscopic statements), and moves their alleged determinism high up the metaphysical ladder.
