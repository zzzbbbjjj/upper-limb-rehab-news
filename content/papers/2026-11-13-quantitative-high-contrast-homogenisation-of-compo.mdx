## English Summary

This work concerns a non-uniformly elliptic second-order differential operator with periodic coefficients that describes a composite medium formed by a periodic array of highly anisotropic cylindrical fibres set in an isotropic matrix. The ratio of transverse to axial conductivity in the fibres is linked to the period of the coefficients through a `critical' high-contrast scaling, and the axial conductivity is allowed to vary along the fibres. As the period $\varepsilon$ tends to zero, ellipticity degenerates in the directions transverse to the fibres. We are concerned with the resolvent of this operator in the small-$\varepsilon$ limit. For fibres with constant axial conductivity, the two-scale limit of such problems was identified by Cherednichenko, Smyshlyaev and Zhikov (Proceedings of the Royal Society of Edinburgh: Section A Mathematics 136(1), 87--114 (2006)), and a full two-scale asymptotic expansion, with remainder estimates for smooth right-hand sides, was constructed by Cherednichenko (Asymptotic Analysis 49(1--2), 39--59 (2006)). In the non-constant axial conductivity setting, considered here, we approximate the resolvent in terms of the two-scale limit operator and prove that the error is order $\varepsilon$ in the operator norm, uniformly over right-hand sides in the unit ball of $L^2(\mathbb{R}^3)$; we also obtain order-$\varepsilon$ estimates for the reciprocals of the spectral band functions. This work builds on results announced, without proof, for fibres with circular cross-section in Cooper and Kamotski (Direct and Inverse Problems with Applications: Extended Abstracts of the 2024 GAP Center Summer School, pp.~69--75, Birkhäuser (2026)).
