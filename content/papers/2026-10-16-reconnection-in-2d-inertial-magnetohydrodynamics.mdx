## 中文摘要

我们在两个空间维度上考虑惯性磁流体动力学的双流体方程。这些方程可以重写为两个活动标量的演化，具有类似流体的相互作用核。这些方程有两种形式：左手方程和右手方程。它们是保守的，并且对于平滑初始数据和 Yudovich 类 $L^1(\mathbb R^2)\cap L^{\infty}(\mathbb R^2)$ 中的初始数据都有全局唯一的解决方案。我们证明了无粘极限在尤多维奇级中成立。我们通过左手方程的合并事件证明了斑块边界 $C^{2,α}$ 规律性的持久性。我们最终展示了磁重联的数值示例，验证了已知的严格结果并发现了新的案例。

## English Summary

We consider two-fluid equations of inertial magnetohydrodynamics in two spatial dimensions. These equations can be recast as the evolution of two active scalars, with fluid-like interaction kernels. The equations come in two flavors, left-handed and right-handed. They are conservative and have global unique solutions for both smooth initial data and for initial data in Yudovich class $L^1(\mathbb R^2)\cap L^{\infty}(\mathbb R^2)$. We prove that the inviscid limit holds in Yudovich class. We prove the persistence of $C^{2,α}$ regularity of patch boundaries through merger events for the left-handed equations. We finally show numerical examples of magnetic reconnection, validating known rigorous results and uncovering new cases.
