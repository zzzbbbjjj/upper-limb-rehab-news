## English Summary

We study the two-dimensional compressible Navier-Stokes-Poisson system in a general simply connected bounded domain with Navier-slip boundary conditions and a positive, possibly non-flat, doping profile. We establish the global existence of a unique strong solution and of a global weak solution for arbitrarily large initial data allowing vacuum when the shear viscosity is a positive constant and the bulk one proportional to a power of the density with the power bigger than 4/3.   The main difficulties arise from the interaction between the Poisson force and the general boundary geometry. To overcome them, we introduce a modified material derivative and derive quantitative $L^p$ estimates for the density by means of a Neumann duality argument together with a harmonic correction generated by the Navier-slip boundary condition. We further obtain a pointwise representation of the effective viscous flux by pulling back the Neumann Green function on the unit disk through a conformal mapping. The resulting commutator estimates, combined with elliptic estimates for the Poisson equation, yield a uniform-in-time upper bound for the density and allow us to close the higher-order a priori estimates. Finally, the global weak solution is constructed through a charge-preserving approximation and compactness argument.
