## 中文摘要

我们考虑自对偶杨米尔斯和自对偶引力的标量作用。我们给出了它们的无限对称塔的新颖恢复版本。我们证明，对应于无限塔的各个能级的对称性沃德恒等式给出了波函数系数的软极限，而它们的恢复版本完全固定了整个波函数。我们的分析对于一般边界条件是有效的。最后，我们给出了 $n$ 点波函数系数的显式公式，作为重组扰动器解的单个行列式，该行列式也满足软极限。

## English Summary

We consider the scalar actions for self-dual Yang-Mills and self-dual gravity. We give a novel resummed version of their infinite tower of symmetries. We show that the Ward identities for symmetries corresponding to the individual levels of the infinite tower give the soft limits for wavefunction coefficients, while their resummed version completely fixes the full wavefunction. Our analysis is valid for general boundary conditions. Finally, we give an explicit formula for the $n$-point wavefunction coefficient as a single determinant reorganising the perturbiner solution, which also satisfies the soft limits.
