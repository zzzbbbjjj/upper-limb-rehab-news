## English Summary

Let $E$ be the Cantor set, $G=\operatorname{Homeo}(E)$, let $P\le G$ be the group of order preserving homeomorphisms and $L\le G$ the group of homeomorphisms preserving the Cantor--Lebesgue measure $λ$. We study how $G$ is built from these two subgroups. In 2003, Glasner and Weiss stated that $G=LP=PL$. We show that this is not the case, in fact $PL$ and $LP$ are two different proper subsets of $G$. On the other hand, $PL$ is a dense $G_δ$ subset of $G$, the multiplication map $P\times L\to PL$ is a homeomorphism, and $G=PLP=LPL$. In particular, every homeomorphism of $E$ is conjugate to an element of $PL$. We classify the double cosets $PgL$ using special clopen values set. There are continuum many of them, and $PL$ is the only one which is not meager. We prove that $G$ is topologically generated by one element of $P$ and one element of $L$ and these two generators produce the Thompson's group $V$. We also show that the generic homeomorphism of $E$ is the product of a generic element of $P$ and a generic element of $L$. The equality $G=PL$ fails also if $λ$ is replaced by any other full nonatomic measure.
