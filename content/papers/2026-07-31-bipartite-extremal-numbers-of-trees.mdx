## 中文摘要

我们研究了经典的 Erdős-Sós 问题（树的极值数量）对二分主图类的限制，无论是当仅规定主的顺序还是当其两个部分大小固定时。我们给出自然的下界构造并制定相应的线性上限猜想。我们应用$k$-极小性的加权变体来证明一系列树的上限，包括扫帚、部分大小最多相差一个的树以及最多 7 个顶点的所有树，从而解决了 Caro、Patkós 和 Tuza 问题的一部分直至加性常数。我们还将树的固定部分极值数与普通极值数联系起来，并考虑类似于 Zarankiewicz 函数的有向二分极值函数。

## English Summary

We study a restriction of the classical Erdős--Sós problem, the extremal number of trees, to the class of bipartite host graphs, both when only the order of the host is prescribed and when its two part-sizes are fixed. We give natural lower-bound constructions and formulate corresponding linear upper-bound conjectures. We apply a weighted variant of $k$-minimality to prove upper bounds for a broad family of trees including brooms, trees with part-sizes differing by at most one, and all trees on at most 7 vertices, resolving part of a problem of Caro, Patkós and Tuza up to additive constants. We also relate the fixed-part extremal number of a tree to the ordinary extremal number, and consider an oriented bipartite extremal function analogous to the Zarankiewicz function.
