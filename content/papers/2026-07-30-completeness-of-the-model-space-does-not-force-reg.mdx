## 中文摘要

我们构建了一个反例来解决无限维李理论中的一个基本开放问题：在完全局部凸空间上建模的每个李群是否都是正则的。这个例子是一个可收缩复解析BCH——李群$H$，以完整席尔瓦空间$\varphi_{\mathbb C}$为模型，其指数映射是同胚，但$H$甚至不是$C^0$-半正则。在一个固定的有限维子空间中趋向于零的平滑控制没有 $H$ 值的 $C^1$ 演化，尽管每个控制在 $[0,1)$ 上都有独特的平滑演化。群是完备复连续逆代数的主要单位群。该构造还否定地回答了 Glöckner-Neeb 的问题，即即使在更强的完备性假设下，它们的乘法增长条件对于 Mackey 完备的连续逆代数是否是自动的。

## English Summary

We construct a counterexample resolving a fundamental open problem in infinite-dimensional Lie theory: whether every Lie group modelled on a complete locally convex space is regular. The example is a contractible complex analytic BCH--Lie group $H$, modelled on the complete Silva space $\varphi_{\mathbb C}$, whose exponential map is a homeomorphism, yet $H$ is not even $C^0$-semiregular. Smooth controls tending to zero in one fixed finite-dimensional subspace have no $H$-valued $C^1$ evolution, although each has a unique smooth evolution on $[0,1)$. The group is the principal unit group of a complete complex continuous inverse algebra. The construction also answers negatively the Glöckner--Neeb question whether their multiplication-growth condition is automatic for Mackey-complete continuous inverse algebras, even under the stronger assumption of completeness.
