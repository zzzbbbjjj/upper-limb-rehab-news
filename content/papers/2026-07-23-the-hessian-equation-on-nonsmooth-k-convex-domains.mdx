## 中文摘要

对于$k>n/2$，证明对于任何有限Borel 测度（非光滑且非均匀）$k$-凸（也称为$k$-超凸）域上的$k$-Hessian 测度的狄利克雷问题，作为取自域闭包上的$k$-凸和连续函数的右侧和边界数据，具有唯一解。对于严格的 $k$ 凸域（即，如果存在强障碍），仅连续边界数据就足够了。

## English Summary

It is proved for $k&gt;n/2$ that the Dirichlet problem of the $k$-Hessian measure on a (nonsmooth and nonuniformly) $k$-convex (also known as $k$-hyperconvex) domain for any finite Borel measure as right-hand side and boundary data taken from a $k$-convex and continuous function on the closure of the domain has a unique solution. Merely continuous boundary data are sufficient for strictly $k$-convex domains (i.e., if there exist strong barriers).
