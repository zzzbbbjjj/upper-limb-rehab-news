## English Summary

We prove conjectured-optimal large-time upper decay estimates for solutions of weakly coupled systems of $m\ge2$ time-fractional diffusion equations. Our result establishes the upper-bound assertion in the conjecture of Li, Liu, and Wada~\cite{LLW26}. The sharpness of these upper exponents for $m\ge3$ remains open in general; see Section~\ref{sec:discussion}.   Assume that the diffusion matrices are uniformly elliptic, the coupling coefficients are bounded and may depend on time, and the matrix of $L^\infty$-norms of the coupling coefficients satisfies a spectral-radius stability condition. Let $α^*$ denote the smallest fractional order among the components with nonzero initial data. Then the $L^2(Ω)^m$-norm of the solution is $O(t^{-α^*})$ as $t\to\infty$ if $α^*&lt;1$, whereas it is $O(t^{-(1+α_m)})$ if $α^*=1$, where $α_m$ is the smallest fractional order occurring in the system. For $m=2$, the result yields the same upper decay exponents as \cite[Theorem~1.1]{LLW26}; in the absence of self-coupling, the matrix stability condition is weaker than the corresponding small-coupling condition in that work. The case $m\ge3$ is new.   Our proof uses a large-time weighted Volterra framework. If $α^*&lt;1$, the weight is a regularly varying power weight; if $α^*=1$, it is constructed by convolution with the resolvent kernel associated with the smallest fractional order. These weights yield the required large-time bounds for all resolvent kernels in the system. In particular, the argument avoids the Laplace-transform and residue-calculus methods used in \cite{LLW26}, which become difficult to implement for systems with three or more components.
