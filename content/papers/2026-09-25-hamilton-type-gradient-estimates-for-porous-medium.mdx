## English Summary

We establish local Hamilton-type gradient estimates for positive $C^{2,1}$ solutions of $u_t=Δu^m$ on complete Riemannian manifolds whose Ricci curvature is bounded from below. For every fixed $m&gt;1$ and every fixed $0&lt;m&lt;1$, there exist $β=β(m,n)&gt;0$ and $C=C(m,n)&gt;0$ such that a solution $0&lt;u\leqslant A$ in $B_{2R}(x_0)\times(t_0-T,t_0]$ satisfies the following local gradient estimate \begin{equation*} \sup_{B_R(x_0)\times(t_0-T/2,t_0]}|\nabla u^β| \leqslant C A^β\left(\frac1R+\sqrt{k}+\frac{A^{(1-m)/2}}{\sqrt T}\right), \end{equation*} where $\mathrm{Ric}_M\geqslant-k$. The proof uses an intrinsic quantitative alternative to locate, around each prescribed positive point, a cylinder on which the solution has a controlled upper-to-lower ratio. A local gradient estimate on this cylinder is combined with a stopping argument. As a consequence, every uniformly bounded positive ancient solution on a connected complete manifold with nonnegative Ricci curvature is constant.
