## English Summary

Using spectral and polynomial methods, we show several sharp spectral bounds for the clique and chromatic numbers of graph powers that only use spectral information from the original graph. In particular, we strengthen earlier spectral-radius estimates to obtain an asymptotically sharp colouring bound for general graph powers, and prove a sharp bound for powers of line graphs with a complete characterisation of equality. We also develop polynomial methods yielding upper and lower bounds for clique numbers of graph powers. Regarding the connection between the clique and coloring parameters of graph powers, we propose a spectral analogue of Wegner's conjecture, determine the extremal values for graph squares, and establish the conjecture for all powers when the spectral-radius bound is sufficiently small. Finally, we connect our spectral polynomial method with coding theory: we prove that for distance-regular graphs, optimisation over the adjacency algebra recovers Delsarte's linear programming bound for anticodes.
