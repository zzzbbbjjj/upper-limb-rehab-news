## 中文摘要

限制在液滴中的活性粒子可以使其宿主液滴运动。这种现象通常归因于封闭颗粒的直接流体动力作用。在这里，我们揭示了一种不同的机制：我们表明，被限制在水面薄油滴中的自驱动活性胶体筏通常遵循偏心轨道，从而以各向异性方式周期性地使油滴的接触线变形。这种不可逆的变形起到游动的作用，并根据低雷诺数游动的原理推动液滴。这种效应对于单一胶体来说是不存在的，而是由筏本身的多体动力学引起的，我们通过耦合粘性流体动力学和多体主动动力学的动力学模型来捕获该效应。我们的结果表明集体内部活动如何通过软限制边界的变形转化为运动。

## English Summary

Active particles confined in droplets can set their host droplet into motion. This phenomenon is usually attributed to direct hydrodynamic forcing by the enclosed particles. Here we reveal a different mechanism: we show that a self-propelled raft of active colloids confined in a thin oil droplet at the surface of water typically follows an off-centered orbit, thus periodically deforming the droplet's contact line in an anisotropic way. This non-reciprocal deformation acts as a swimming stroke and propels the droplet according to the principles of low-Reynolds-number swimming. The effect is absent for a single colloid and arises from the intrinsically many-body dynamics of the raft, which we capture with a dynamical model coupling viscous hydrodynamics and many-body active dynamics. Our results show how collective internal activity can be converted into locomotion through deformation of a soft confining boundary.
