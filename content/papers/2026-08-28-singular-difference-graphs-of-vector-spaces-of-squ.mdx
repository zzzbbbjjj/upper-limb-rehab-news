## English Summary

The singular difference graph, denoted by $Γ$, of the vector space of square matrices over a field   is a graph whose vertex set is the set of   all elements of the vector space, where two distinct vertices are adjacent if and only if the difference   of the corresponding matrices is singular. In this paper, we investigate fundamental   graph-theoretic properties of $Γ$, including connectivity, diameter, regularity,   the Eulerian property, independence number, clique number, and domination   number. We show that $Γ$ is a connected regular graph with diameter two. Over finite   fields, we obtain an explicit formula for the   degree of each vertex and characterize precisely when $Γ$ is   Eulerian. We determine the independence number and clique number and   provide explicit constructions attaining these values using companion   matrices of irreducible polynomials. We also construct an explicit   dominating set, yielding an upper bound for the domination number.
