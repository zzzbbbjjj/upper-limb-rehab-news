## 中文摘要

我们研究具有奇异线性部分的线性二次向量方程组解的存在性。我们推导了右侧较小的充分条件。

## English Summary

We study the existence of solutions for systems of linear-quadratic vector equations with singular linear parts. We derive a sufficient condition for small right hand sides.
