## 中文摘要

令$E$ 为在$\mathbb{Q}$ 上定义的椭圆曲线。进一步假设 $E$ 在 $p=2$ 处具有良好的普通约简。在本文中，我们证明了格林伯格关于当 $E$ 具有 2 次有理同源性时代数 Iwasawa $μ$-不变量的值的猜想，并获得了所有此类曲线的 $2$-adic $μ$-不变量的尖锐绝对上限。我们还证明了 Coates 和 Sujatha 在 $p=2$ 时引入的精细 Selmer 群的代数 $μ$ 不变量的消失。

## English Summary

Let $E$ be an elliptic curve define over $\mathbb{Q}$. Further assume $E$ has good ordinary reduction at $p=2$. In this article, we prove Greenberg's conjecture on the value of the algebraic Iwasawa $μ$-invariant when $E$ has a rational isogeny of degree 2, and obtain a sharp absolute upper bound of the $2$-adic $μ$-invariants for all such curves. We also prove the vanishing of the algebraic $μ$-invariant of the fine Selmer group introduced by Coates and Sujatha at $p=2$.
