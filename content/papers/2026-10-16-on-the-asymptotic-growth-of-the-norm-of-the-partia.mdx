## 中文摘要

我们证明狄利克雷级数 Hardy 空间 $\mathscr H^1$ 上的部分和算子 $S_N$ 的范数满足 $$\liminf_{N\to\infty}\frac{\log\log N}{\log N}\lVert S_N\rVert_{\mathscr H^1\to\mathscr H^1}\geq\frac{1-\log 2}{2π}.$$ 结合 Bondarenko、Brevig、Saksman 和 Seip 的上限，这表明 $$\lVert S_N\rVert_{\mathscr H^1\to\mathscr H^1}\asymp\frac{\log N}{\log\log N}$$...

## English Summary

We show that the norm of the partial sum operator $S_N$ on the Hardy space $\mathscr H^1$ of Dirichlet series satisfies $$\liminf_{N\to\infty}\frac{\log\log N}{\log N}\lVert S_N\rVert_{\mathscr H^1\to\mathscr H^1}\geq\frac{1-\log 2}{2π}.$$ Combined with an upper bound of Bondarenko, Brevig, Saksman and Seip, this shows that $$\lVert S_N\rVert_{\mathscr H^1\to\mathscr H^1}\asymp\frac{\log N}{\log\log N}$$ as $N\to\infty$.
