## English Summary

We study streaming codes for a three-node relay network with burst lengths $b_1,b_2$ and an end-to-end decoding delay $T$. Let $u=\max\{b_1,b_2\}$ and $v=\min\{b_1,b_2\}$, and write $T-v=pu+q$, where $T\ge u+v$ and $0\le q&lt;u$. We develop two binary constructions, SR and SR-2, using extended delay profiles that allow the relay to forward causally invertible combinations of current and past source symbols. The SR construction attains the non-adaptive rate upper bound whenever \[ \frac{T-u-v}{2u-v}\le\left\lfloor\frac{T-u-v}{u}\right\rfloor. \] SR-2 enlarges this range under the sufficient condition \[ q(v-q)\le(p-1)u(u-v). \] These constructions complement the known divisibility and large-delay regimes. We also establish two limitations of systematic ordinary coordinate forwarding. First, it cannot attain the rate upper bound when $0&lt;T-u-v&lt;v$. Second, for equal burst lengths $b$, it attains the bound if and only if $b\mid(T-2b)$; a quantitative upper bound gives a strictly positive rate gap in every nondivisible case. These converses allow nonlinear components and predetermined time variation within the stated ordinary-forwarding architecture. Existing adaptive schemes for equal bursts approach the same rate upper bound asymptotically using time-varying convolutional codes and lie outside the non-adaptive setting considered here.
