## English Summary

We introduce an approach to solving locally ordered quantum spin systems using neural network quantum states. First, our approach rests on choosing a spin basis constructed from the magnetic texture of a classical or quantum reference state. Second, the method improves convergence and suppresses parameter-space drift through a regularized Riemannian gradient descent adapted to the quantum state geometry of the variational state manifold. Combining these techniques enables stable ground-state calculations for two- and three-dimensional chiral spin systems with Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya interactions. The resulting variational ground-state energies are competitive with those of high-fidelity density matrix renormalization group calculations and, for periodic and layered geometries, provide significantly tighter upper bounds on the ground-state energy.
