## 中文摘要

我们证明，对于 Steinhaus 随机乘法函数或 Rademacher 随机乘法函数 $f$，以及每个 $\eps>0$，几乎可以肯定 $$ \left|\sum_{n\le x}f(n)\right|\ll_{\eps,f}\sqrt{x}(\log\log x)^{1/4+\eps}。 $$ 与哈珀几乎确定的下限一起，这决定了两个模型中的锐对数指数。这解决了哈珀关于随机乘法函数大波动的猜想。

## English Summary

We prove that, for either a Steinhaus random multiplicative function or a Rademacher random multiplicative function $f$, and every $\eps&gt;0$, almost surely $$ \left|\sum_{n\le x}f(n)\right|\ll_{\eps,f}\sqrt{x}(\log\log x)^{1/4+\eps}. $$ Together with Harper's almost sure lower bound, this determines the sharp logarithmic exponent in both models. This settles Harper's conjecture on the large fluctuations of random multiplicative functions.
