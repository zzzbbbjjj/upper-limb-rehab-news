## English Summary

Let $A$ be Hermitian positive definite and let $f_m$ denote the Lanczos approximation to $f(A)b$. We prove that if $f(z)$ or $f(z) / z$ is Stieltjes, then the $A^α$-norm error of the Lanczos approximation is within a factor $\tfrac{1}{2}(κ(A)^{E/2} + κ(A)^{-E/2})$ of the the best possible Krylov Subspace Method, where $κ(A)$ is the condition number of $A$ and $E = \max\{α,1-α\}$. Our result strengthens and generalizes the upper bound of [Schweitzer; SIMAX, 46.3 (2025)]. Moreover, we prove that the constant $\tfrac{1}{2}(κ(A)^{E/2} + κ(A)^{-E/2})$ is optimal.
