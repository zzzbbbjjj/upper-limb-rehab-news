## 中文摘要

我们证明了紧支撑测度的正交和径向投影的上下中间尺寸的锐界。当准 Assouad 维数不超过目标维数时，这两个维数函数几乎在任何地方都被保留。我们使用整个源维度函数获得例外集估计，并具有显式族的清晰度。我们还构建了一个固定测度，即使所有源维度都低于目标维度，其投影维度函数也会在开放方向集上变化。

## English Summary

We prove sharp bounds for the upper and lower intermediate dimensions of orthogonal and radial projections of compactly supported measures. Both dimension functions are preserved almost everywhere when the quasi-Assouad dimension does not exceed the target dimension. We obtain exceptional-set estimates using the entire source dimension function, with sharpness for explicit families. We also construct a fixed measure whose projected dimension functions vary on open direction sets, even when all source dimensions lie below the target dimension.
