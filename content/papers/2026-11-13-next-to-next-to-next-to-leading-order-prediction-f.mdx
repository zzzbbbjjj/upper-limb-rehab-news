## English Summary

We accomplish for the first time the three-loop computation of the photonic radiative jet function in soft-collinear effective theory, which constitutes the last missing and most difficult ingredient for the complete next-to-next-to-next-to-leading-order QCD prediction of the simplest radiative $B_{u}^- \to γ\ell^{-} \bar ν_{\ell}$ decay with an energetic photon at leading power. Importantly, the most prominent three-loop contribution to the radiative bottom-meson decay form factors turns out to arise from the newly determined QCD correction to the jet function rather than from the recently obtained hard correction. We further demonstrate explicitly that our full three-loop calculation of these non-hadronic radiative $B$-meson decay form factors appear to be highly beneficial for an improved extraction of the fundamental shape parameter $λ_B$ of the leading-twist $B$-meson light-cone distribution amplitude.
