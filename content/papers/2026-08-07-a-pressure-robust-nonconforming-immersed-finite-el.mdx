## 中文摘要

众所周知，对测试函数的适当修改可能会导致针对斯托克斯问题的压力鲁棒混合方法。然而，对于未拟合网格上斯托克斯界面问题的浸没有限元近似，仍不清楚速度误差是否与压力无关，因为速度和压力在界面条件之一中耦合。在本文中，我们通过将不连续压力新颖地分解为连续分量和速度相关的不连续分量，提供了肯定的答案。我们证明了浸入式 Crouzeix--Raviart/$P_0$ 单元方法通过对右侧测试函数进行符合 $H(\operatorname{div})$ 的重构来实现压力鲁棒性。该方法的稳定性和最佳误差估计是用与网格相对的界面位置无关的常数建立的。提出了数值实验来验证理论结果。

## English Summary

It is well established that an appropriate modification of test functions may lead to pressure-robust mixed methods for Stokes problems. However, for immersed finite element approximations of Stokes interface problems on unfitted meshes, it remains unclear whether the velocity error is independent of the pressure, since the velocity and the pressure are coupled in one of the interface conditions. In this paper, we provide a positive answer through a novel decomposition of the discontinuous pressure into a continuous component and a velocity-dependent discontinuous component. We demonstrate that the immersed Crouzeix--Raviart/$P_0$ element method achieves pressure robustness via an $H(\operatorname{div})$-conforming reconstruction of the test functions on the right-hand side. The stability and optimal error estimates of the proposed method are established with constants independent of the interface position relative to the mesh. Numerical experiments are presented to validate the theoretical findings.
