## 中文摘要

我们用左手切割概括了最近提出的有效范围扩展[Phys。莱特牧师。 135, 011903 (2025)]耦合通道框架。通过系统地分析不同质量配置下 $S$ 波耦合通道单粒子交换 (OPE) 势的奇点结构，我们得出了耦合通道散射幅度的解析参数化，其中包含多通道的 OPE 左手切割。以双通道情况为例，我们通过比较不同展开阶数下的幅度及其倒数，将参数化与 Lippmann-Schwinger 方程 (LSE) 进行比较。即使在最低阶，只有六个参数，耦合通道参数化也能忠实地再现 LSE 结果，并能够精确提取耦合常数，证明其对于涉及粒子交换的二体耦合通道散射问题的可靠性。

## English Summary

We generalize the recently proposed effective-range expansion with a left-hand cut [Phys. Rev. Lett. 135, 011903 (2025)] to a coupled-channel framework. By systematically analyzing the singularity structure of the $S$-wave coupled-channel one-particle exchange (OPE) potential under various mass configurations, we derive an analytical parametrization of the coupled-channel scattering amplitude that incorporates the OPE left-hand cut for multiple channels. Taking the two-channel case as an illustration, we benchmark this parametrization against the Lippmann--Schwinger equation (LSE) by comparing the amplitudes and their inverses at different expansion orders. Even at the lowest order, with only six parameters, the coupled-channel parametrization faithfully reproduces the LSE results and enables precise extraction of the coupling constants, demonstrating its reliability for two-body coupled-channel scattering problems involving particle exchange.
