## 中文摘要

令 $-A$ 为 $σ$-有限测度空间上的实对称收缩半群的生成元。我们证明了最大 $\mathrm{L}^p$ 正则性的通用界限： 在时空范数 $$\|Au\|_p \leq \big(p^* + \tfrac{p^*}{2(p^*-1)}\big) \|f\|_p,$$ 中，$p^* = \max(p,p/(p-1))$ 和 $u$ 是柯西问题的温和解右侧 $f$ 从 $u(0)=0$ 开始。 $p^*$ 中常数的线性增长是最佳的。我们的证明引入了一种最大正则性的基本方法，避免了向量值调和分析和膨胀理论。我们还获得了在扇区上收缩的实非对称半群的定量最大正则性估计。

## English Summary

Let $-A$ be the generator of a real, symmetric contraction semigroup on a $σ$-finite measure space. We prove a universal bound for maximal $\mathrm{L}^p$-regularity: In space-time norms $$\|Au\|_p \leq \big(p^* + \tfrac{p^*}{2(p^*-1)}\big) \|f\|_p,$$ where $p^* = \max(p,p/(p-1))$ and $u$ is the mild solution to the Cauchy problem with right-hand side $f$ starting at $u(0)=0$. Linear growth of the constant in $p^*$ is optimal. Our proof introduces an elementary approach to maximal regularity that avoids both vector-valued harmonic analysis and dilation theory. We also obtain quantitative maximal regularity estimates for real, non-symmetric semigroups that are contractive on a sector.
